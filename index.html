What the numbers mean and what they don’t

Fair is the current score plus a rest-of-game estimate fitted on 16,086 Division I games from 2022–23 through December 2025. It uses the closing total and spread, how far each team is ahead of or behind the pace those lines implied, and the lead.

The model knows nothing about injuries, foul trouble or who is on the floor. A large gap can mean the book knows something. ESPN’s feeds are unofficial and can lag or change. This is information, not advice.
