What three seasons say

In 16,086 games since 2022, a top-50 team six or more points behind pace at halftime had lost 9.5 points and got 0.6 back. From there it scored at 101.6% of its pregame pace, across 831 team-games.

So a fair live total is the score plus a normal pace for the time left. There is an edge only when the book prices the cold to continue.

Game totals are settled at DraftKings’ live price. Team totals are implied from the live total and spread, and priced at an estimated -115.

Scores and play-by-play come from ESPN. Lines are DraftKings’ pregame and live numbers as ESPN publishes them. ESPN drops the live line at the final whistle, so a recorder saves it every 30 seconds while games are on.

Fair is the current score plus a rest-of-game estimate fitted on 16,086 Division I games, 2022–23 through December 2025. It knows nothing about injuries, foul trouble or who is on the floor. Gaps are measured in deviations: the usual distance between book and fair for the time left, re-estimated from every completed slate. This is information, not betting advice.
